# Reconciliation review

A review passes only when the reviewer independently verifies all applicable checks against the exact evidence and result digests.

## Population and basis

- entity, accounts, currencies, cutoff, grain, date basis, sign convention, and source authority are explicit;
- both sides use the same gross/net, live/as-of, and currency basis;
- control components sum to the control;
- every source row is accounted for exactly once;
- duplicates, missing identifiers, pagination/completeness limits, and exclusions are surfaced;
- source totals agree to matched values plus all unmatched populations;
- supplied workbook sheets were checked for containment before aggregation.

## Matching and arithmetic

- keys and secondary rules were approved before use;
- Decimal arithmetic is used for monetary comparison;
- the threshold was approved before the result was observed;
- control algebra was simplified to expose cancelled terms;
- the gate tests the published figure;
- membership and per-document amounts were both checked where a benchmark exists.

## Reconciling items

- material breaks have evidence-backed causes or remain explicitly unresolved;
- every named item has amount, cutoff, owner, ruling identity/date, rationale, and evidence;
- named items are shown separately and do not alter raw source totals;
- residuals before and after named items are visible;
- old items are aged and retired under the approved policy;
- no tolerance was widened or item introduced merely to force a pass.

## Evidence and publication

- each attestation proves the fields and population claimed rather than only file existence or shape;
- one control or attestation does not bypass another;
- blocked controls state why, who owns the remedy, and what happens next;
- preparer and reviewer are different identities;
- the reviewer recomputed from source evidence rather than approving a summary;
- the published artifact was opened and its headline figures, labels, attestations, exclusions, basis, and gating control were read;
- method changes that convert a block to publication have explicit finance approval.

An approval label without these checks and their evidence is not a review.
