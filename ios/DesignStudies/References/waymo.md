## Observed

The pickup screen pairs a neutral, pale map with a white rounded panel. A small gray time pill sits above a confident status heading. One large bright-blue action dominates; its secondary action has a quiet gray outline. The start-ride screen repeats that structure, followed by a separate vehicle-information card. Simple outlined navigation symbols sit above small labels. The App Store asset's blue background is declared as RGB 0, 113, 241; this is used as the study's blue token. The UI's sans-serif has open, rounded forms; its exact font is not established by this research.

## Interpretation for QM

QM keeps Chats, search, active and recent conversations, New chat, and a standard transcript/composer. Pale blue space, white panels, rounded typography, quiet secondary information, and clear blue actions carry the visual language. No map or ride-booking structure is reproduced. Agent steps remain inside the conversation as a functional work disclosure. SF Rounded substitutes for the observed type character without claiming a proprietary font match.

The study includes Chats, Activity, three populated conversations, new chat, local sample replies, sample attachments, expandable agent steps, a model picker, and result sheets. It has no backend connection; content and responses are invented demonstration data. Configuration and persisted/API data shapes are unchanged.
