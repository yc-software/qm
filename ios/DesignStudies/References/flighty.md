## Observed

White and very light gray status cards use near-black primary text, quieter gray metadata, compact informational rows, and strongly differentiated hierarchy. Time and route information dominate; status updates are concise and factual. The official website stage selector is a dark capsule with a bright yellow selected option. Green signals positive progress, yellow identifies attention or gate details, and blue marks routes and primary actions. Official screenshots use a light map with curved routes, while promotional and Live Activity imagery includes charcoal surfaces with saturated accents. The typography visually resembles native system sans serif; no exact font specification was obtained.

## Adaptation and inference

QM retains a chat-first structure: Chats, All/Working/Needs you filters, conversation previews, New chat, and regular transcripts with expandable agent activity. Flighty contributes precise typography, compact metadata, white and light-gray cards, dark neutral framing, blue actions, green statuses, and yellow selection accents. Maps, airport codes, route diagrams, flight metrics, and travel navigation are not reproduced. Spacing, radii, and exact color values are visual approximations rather than measured proprietary tokens.

The study is a local interactive prototype with seeded conversations and simulated responses. Filters, opening chats, agent activity, model selection, attachments, composing, and exit navigation work. No backend, live agent run, Live Activity entitlement, storage schema, or production configuration is changed. Demo messages are intentionally disposable session state.
